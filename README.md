# Taskling: A Gamified To-Do App with a Virtual Pet Companion
Taskling transforms your everyday to-do list into an engaging game where productivity earns you rewards and your virtual pet’s happiness depends on it! Complete tasks, earn coins, and use them to care for and customize your digital companion's world.

## How It Works
#### Complete Tasks
- Stay on track by adding and completing tasks. Each task earns you coins as a reward for staying productive.
#### Earn & Spend Coins
- Use your coins to feed your pet, buy furniture, and unlock fun items to decorate their space.
#### Care for Your Virtual Pet
- Your pet grows happier and healthier as you complete more tasks and use your coins to take care of them.

## Features
- Clean, intuitive task management
- Coin-based reward system to motivate goal completion
- In-app shop for food and decorative items
- Virtual pet that responds to your engagement
- Fun and interactive UI with gamification elements

## Technologies Used
- Frontend: React
- Backend: Node.js with Express API
- Database: MongoDB

## Why We Built Taskling
We created Taskling to explore how gamification can make everyday productivity more fun, motivating, and emotionally engaging. By combining goal-setting with digital care, Taskling encourages users to build better habits while enjoying the journey. Taskling is a collaborative group project built with passion, creativity, and teamwork. We welcome feedback, ideas, or contributions from the community!
